# Data Analytics and Engineering Consultant @ The Information Lab US.

### Currently studying DBT and brushing up on my SQL skills!

I hold a bachelor’s degree in Psychology from Pace University, where I developed strong skills in qualitative and quantitative data analysis through my research into the contributing factors of the development of speaking anxiety in foreign language learners. Prior to working with the Information Lab, I built experience in web design, statistical analysis of product reviews, and wrote blog articles on current events tailored for SEO.

At the Information Lab, I build interactive and intuitive dashboards that make understanding complicated databases easy at any level with actionable insights to improve operations. In the backend, I use my experience with SQL, Snowflake, DBT, Github, and Databricks to ensure that insights are kept relevant to business problems and supported by accurate data.
